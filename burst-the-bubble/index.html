Why it stays fresh

Built to never look the same twice.

No two boards share a layout. Difficulty, hazards, and board shape all scale together as you climb, so level 400 feels like a different game from level 4 — without ever feeling unfair.

🎯

Procedurally infinite

Every board is generated from a unique seed — wavy silhouettes, radial blobs, and noise-carved gaps combine into layouts nobody has seen before.

🧊

Real bubble hazards

Ice bubbles freeze, Fire bubbles chain-burn a row, Lasers clear a band, Bombs go off — and Stone obstacles patrol the board and dare you to route around them.

👑

Boss battles

Every tenth level, a multi-cell boss takes root at the top of the board with its own health bar. Chip it down with splash damage or go for direct hits.

🌪️

Wind, gravity & rotation

Later levels bend the rules — wind curves your shot mid-air, gravity arcs it back down, and some boards physically rotate while you aim.

⚡

Power-ups that matter

Bubble Blaster, Color Switcher, Bomb, and Rainbow bubbles — buy them in the shop, carry them with you, use them exactly when a level demands it.

🎁

Daily rewards & achievements

A real 7-day reward cycle, milestone achievements, and a mascot with 925+ lines who always has something to say about how you're doing.

Under the hood

No two boards are rolled the same way.

Instead of picking from a handful of fixed templates, each level layers a randomized wave boundary, an optional radial blob, and a noise-hole mask — then decides on the spot whether this board rotates, drifts in the wind, or sags under gravity. The result: a pattern space that's effectively unlimited, with difficulty that climbs gradually and deliberately as you go.
