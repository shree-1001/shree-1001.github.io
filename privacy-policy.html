01
Information We Collect

Shree Games & Apps is committed to your privacy. Most of our apps are designed to work fully offline and collect minimal or no personal data.

Data stored locally on your device:

  • Player name (if entered by you)
  • High scores and game progress
  • App preferences and settings
  • Levels unlocked or completed

This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. We do not have access to it.

Data collected automatically (via third-party SDKs):

  • Android Advertising ID (for ad personalization)
  • Device model and OS version
  • General location (country/region level only)
  • App usage and session data
  • IP address (used for regional ad targeting)

This data is collected by third-party services (such as Google AdMob) and is governed by their respective privacy policies.

02
How We Use Information

We use collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve our apps and games
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To analyze aggregate usage patterns (non-personal)
  • To fix bugs and optimize performance

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

03
Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some of our apps display advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized ads based on your interests.

AdMob may collect: Advertising ID, device identifiers, IP address, app usage data, and location data (at country level). This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Types of ads we show:

  • Banner — displayed at bottom of screen during gameplay
  • Rewarded — optional full-screen ads that give in-game rewards
  • Interstitial — full-screen ads shown between game sessions

Opt out of personalized ads:

  • Go to Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device
  • Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • Or reset your Advertising ID

Opting out means you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized to your interests.

05
Children's Privacy

Our apps are designed to be suitable for general audiences, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Since we do not collect personal data directly, our apps are inherently safe for younger users. AdMob has been configured to serve ads appropriate for general audiences (G-rated content).

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at shreegamesapps@gmail.com and we will take prompt action.

06
Data Security

Since Shree Games & Apps does not collect or store personal data on our own servers, there is no personal data at risk from our end.

Game data (scores, progress, preferences) stored on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device OS up to date
  • Using a screen lock on your device
  • Only downloading our apps from official stores

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access — Request information about data we hold about you
  • Right to Deletion — Request deletion of your data (local game data can be deleted by uninstalling the app)
  • Right to Opt Out — Opt out of personalized advertising via device settings
  • Right to Portability — Request a copy of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at shreegamesapps@gmail.com.

08
App Permissions

Our apps may request the following device permissions:

  • INTERNET — Required to load advertisements from Google AdMob
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used to check internet connectivity before loading ads
  • VIBRATE (selected apps) — For haptic feedback during gameplay

No other permissions are requested. We never request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files.
