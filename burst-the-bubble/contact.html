Quick answers

I lost my progress after reinstalling. Can I get it back? ✕
Burst the Bubble stores your progress locally on your device rather than in a cloud account, so uninstalling (or clearing app storage) removes it the same way it would for any locally-saved app. We don't have a copy to restore, since we never receive one — see our Privacy Policy for details.
How do I turn off notifications or sound? ✕
Open the in-game Settings screen (gear icon on the Main Menu) — Sound Effects, Background Music, Vibration, and Notifications each have their own toggle, and your choice is remembered.
Can I reset just my stats without losing my level progress? ✕
Yes — the Statistics screen has a "Reset Stats" button that clears combo, accuracy, coins-earned, time played, and streak stats specifically, while leaving your level progress, coins, and owned power-ups untouched.
A level feels impossible — is that a bug? ✕
Levels are procedurally generated and get harder as you climb, but they shouldn't ever feel unfair. If one genuinely seems broken (or you're stuck with no way to finish it), tell us the level number and what happened — that's exactly the kind of report that helps us tune difficulty.
Do you offer refunds for in-app purchases? ✕
All purchases are processed by Google Play, so refunds go through Google Play's own refund policy and process, not through us directly. We're still happy to help if something went wrong — reach out and we'll point you in the right direction.
