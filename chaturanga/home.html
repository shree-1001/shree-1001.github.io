The League System

From Wood to Omnipotent.

One hundred leagues. Every week, the top climbers in your league rise, the bottom fall, and everyone else holds their ground for another round.

How promotion works: each week, the top 30% of your league promotes, the bottom 30% demotes, and the middle 40% stays. Finish in the top three and it's recorded on your profile — permanently.
