How to Play

1

Launch the Game

Open Neon Breakout and enter your player name. No login, no sign-up — just tap and go.

2

Drag to Move

Slide your finger left and right to control the neon paddle at the bottom of the screen.

3

Break All Bricks

Bounce the glowing ball to destroy every brick on the screen. Different bricks, different point values.

4

Beat Your Score

Don't let the ball fall! Clear levels, rack up points, and beat your personal high score.
