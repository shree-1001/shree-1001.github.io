Privacy Policy

How Tratakam handles information — in plain language, before your session begins.

Effective: September 17, 2026 App: Tratakam (com.shree.tratakam)

01Overview

Tratakam is a meditation and focus-training app for candle gazing (trāṭaka), mantra japa, and related focus exercises. It does not require an account, a login, or your name, email address, or phone number to use. This policy explains the little the app collects directly, what our advertising partner collects on its own behalf, and the choices available to you.

02What we collect directly

Tratakam does not ask you to create a profile, and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location to let you use the app.

03Data stored on your device

Your session history, blink counts, streaks, Dhyan Points balance, reminder times, and language/app preferences are saved in a local database on your own phone. This information:

  • Never leaves your device or gets transmitted to us — there is no account system and no backend server
  • Isn't tied to your identity in any way
  • Is removed automatically if you uninstall the app, or clear the app's data from your device settings

04Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tratakam shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the app free. AdMob is operated by Google and, like most ad services, may collect information such as your advertising ID, general device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and app interaction data in order to serve and measure ads — including personalized ads, unless you've limited ad tracking on your device.

This happens independently of us

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy, not ours. We don't receive or see the raw data Google collects — we only see aggregate performance reports.

You can read more about how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's main Privacy Policy.

Consent for personalized ads (UMP regions only). In countries and regions where Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) applies — including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other jurisdictions where consent is required for personalized advertising — you will see a consent form when you first open the app. On that form you can choose to allow personalized ads or to receive non-personalized ads instead. If you are in a UMP region, you can later revoke, change, or give consent again at any time from the app's Settings page (Privacy & Consent option). The in-app consent form and Settings control apply only in UMP countries; they are not shown where consent is not required under applicable law.

Outside those regions, and as an additional control everywhere, you can limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads). Declining personalized ads means you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized to your interests.

05Dhyan Points & rewards

Tratakam includes an entirely virtual, in-app points system ("Dhyan Points"), earned by completing meditation sessions and, optionally, by choosing to watch a rewarded video ad. Points can be spent on cosmetic unlocks such as flame colors, gazing targets, and profile titles, or used for streak repair.

Tratakam does not process any real-money purchases. There is no in-app store involving actual currency, no payment information is collected by the app, and no billing library is used. Rewarded ads are always optional — you choose whether to watch one.

07Children's privacy

Tratakam is not directed at, or designed for, children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll remove it. Because ads are served by a third party, parents of younger users may want to review Google's guidance on ads and children's privacy directly.

08Sharing & selling of data

We do not sell personal information, and we don't share any personal data with third parties beyond what's necessary for Google AdMob to serve ads within the app, as described above.

09Your choices

  • Personalized ads (UMP regions) — where Google UMP applies, use the in-app consent form on first launch, and later revoke or change your choice in the app's Settings page. Outside UMP regions that form is not shown.
  • Reset or limit your advertising ID — in your Android device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads), you can opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising identifier in any region.
  • Turn off reminders — disable daily notifications any time from the app's Settings page.
  • Clear local app data — uninstall the app, or clear its storage from Settings → Apps → Tratakam → Storage, to remove all locally saved sessions, streaks, and points.
  • Contact us — reach out with any privacy question or request using the details below.

10Retention & security

Because your session and progress data stays on your device rather than on our servers, retention and deletion are largely in your control through your device settings. We take reasonable measures within the app to avoid unnecessary data handling in the first place — the simplest safeguard is collecting as little as possible.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves — for example, if a future version adds new features. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective" date at the top of this page.
