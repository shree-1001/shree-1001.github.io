NEON BREAKOUT
PRIVACY POLICY
Effective Date: 1 April, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Neon Breakout ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile game application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the Google Play Families Policy, applicable child privacy laws, and other relevant data protection regulations where applicable.

1. Information We Collect

Neon Breakout is an offline game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The only data stored by the app is:

  • Your high scores and player name — stored locally on your device only
  • Your game progress (levels unlocked) — stored locally on your device only

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Neon Breakout displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (Android Ad ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage data

This data is collected by Google, not by us. Google's use of advertising data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

When the app is used by children, advertisements are intended to comply with the Google Play Families Policy. We use Google AdMob, a Google Play certified advertising network, and configure advertising to help ensure that ads shown to children are appropriate for younger audiences whenever required. We do not serve advertisements ourselves or use advertising networks that are not permitted under the Google Play Families Policy.

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob in accordance with its own privacy practices and applicable legal requirements, including, where applicable, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • Go to Settings → Google → Ads
  • Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

3. Children's Privacy

Neon Breakout is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal data at all, the app is safe for children to use.

Google AdMob has been configured to support compliance with the Google Play Families Policy. Where required, advertisements shown to children are intended to be appropriate for younger audiences and are served only through Google Play certified advertising services.

4. Data Security

Since Neon Breakout does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal data at risk. Your high scores are stored only on your local device and are protected by your device's own security.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party service:

GOOGLE ADMOB — Advertising service by Google LLC
Privacy Policy | Ad Technology

6. Permissions

Neon Breakout may request the following permissions:

  • Internet Access — Required to load advertisements from Google AdMob
  • Network State — Used to check internet connectivity for ads

No other permissions are required or requested.

7. Families Policy Compliance

Neon Breakout is designed to be suitable for players of all ages, including children. We are committed to complying with the Google Play Families Policy and applicable child privacy regulations.

  • Content within the game is intended to be appropriate for children.
  • Advertisements are served through Google AdMob, a Google Play certified advertising network.
  • When required, advertisements shown to children are intended to be appropriate for younger audiences.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
  • The app and its integrated services are intended to comply with applicable child privacy laws, including COPPA and GDPR where applicable.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If changes affect how children's information or advertising is handled, the updated policy will reflect those changes before they become effective.
