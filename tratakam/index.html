Why Trāṭaka

An Ancient Practice, Gently Modernized

I

Rooted in tradition

Trāṭaka — steady, unblinking gazing — is one of the six classical shatkarmas of hatha yoga, practiced for centuries to sharpen concentration and quiet the mind.

II

Made for daily life

Tratakam keeps the practice simple: pick a gazing object, set a duration, and begin. No account, no clutter, no pressure — just a few quiet minutes.

III

Comfort comes first

An in-session eye-care overlay, blink reminders, and an environment guide help you practice safely and comfortably, session after session.

IV

Private by design

No account, no sign-in. Your sessions, streaks, and settings stay on your device. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

A Gentle Note

Tratakam's candle mode uses a softly flickering flame animation. If you're sensitive to flickering or strobing visuals, we'd recommend starting with the Bindu (dot) or Crystal Sphere modes instead, or practicing in shorter sessions.
