Privacy Policy

How Neon Dodge handles information — in plain language, before you go anywhere near the grid.

Effective: July 30, 2026 App: Neon Dodge (com.shree.neondodge)

On this page

01Overview

Neon Dodge is a single-player arcade game. It does not require an account, a login, or a name, email address, or phone number to play. This policy explains the little that the app does collect, what our advertising partner collects on its own behalf, and the choices available to you.

02What we collect directly

Neon Dodge does not ask you to create a profile and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal information to let you play.

CategoryCollected by the app?
Name, email, phone numberNot collected

Photos, contacts, filesNot collected
In-game scores, levels, settingsStored locally on your device only — see below

03Data stored on your device

Your high scores, level progress, and in-game settings are saved in a local database on your own phone. This information:

  • Never leaves your device or gets transmitted to us
  • Isn't tied to your identity in any way
  • Is removed automatically if you uninstall the app, or clear the app's data from your device settings

04Advertising (Google AdMob)

Neon Dodge shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the game free. AdMob is operated by Google and, like most ad services, may collect information such as your advertising ID, general device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and app interaction data in order to serve and measure ads — including personalized ads, unless you've limited ad tracking on your device.

This happens independently of us

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy, not ours. We don't receive or see the raw data Google collects — we only see aggregate performance reports.

You can read more about how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's main Privacy Policy.

Neon Dodge does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage permissions.

06Children's privacy

Neon Dodge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll remove it. Because ads are served by a third party, parents of younger players may want to review Google's guidance on ads and children's privacy directly.

07Sharing & selling of data

We do not sell personal information, and we don't share any personal data with third parties beyond what's necessary for Google AdMob to serve ads within the app, as described above.

08Your choices

  • Reset or limit your advertising ID — in your Android device's Settings → Privacy → Ads, you can opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising identifier.
  • Clear local game data — uninstall the app, or clear its storage from Settings → Apps → Neon Dodge → Storage, to remove all locally saved scores and settings.
  • Contact us — reach out with any privacy question or request using the details below.

09Retention & security

Because gameplay data stays on your device rather than on our servers, retention and deletion are largely in your control through your device settings. We take reasonable measures within the app to avoid unnecessary data handling in the first place — the simplest safeguard is collecting as little as possible.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves — for example, if a future version adds new features. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective" date at the top of this page.
