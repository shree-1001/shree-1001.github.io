The Game

One lane of focus. An entire world of neon.

Neon Dodge drops you into an endless synthwave corridor where obstacles rush the screen and only sharp reflexes keep your streak alive. No timers, no pressure to grind — just you, the beat, and the next flag on the horizon.

Clear a run and it unlocks the next stop on the world map, each one a little faster and a little meaner than the last. Beat your own numbers on the on-device leaderboard, or just play for the glow.
