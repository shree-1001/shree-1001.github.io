📋 Overview

Shree Games And Apps ("we," "us," or "our") built Burst the Bubble (the "App") as a free, single-player bubble-shooter game. This policy explains what data the App interacts with, why, and what choices you have. We wrote it to reflect exactly what the App does — no more, no less.

In short: Burst the Bubble does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, or any personal profile, and does not let you chat with other players. Your game progress (level, coins, achievements, settings) is stored locally on your own device. The only outside party the App talks to is Google's advertising network, which serves the optional ads that support the free game.

🗂️ Data We Collect

We (the developer) do not operate our own servers or accounts for this App, so we do not collect or receive:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Passwords or account credentials
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Photos, contacts, or files from your device
  • Payment card details (any in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play's own billing system, which has its own privacy policy)

The App also has no chat, messaging, or user-generated content features, so there's nothing for other players to see about you.

💾 Data Stored On Your Device

To make the game work, the App stores the following only on your device, using Android's local app storage — this data is never transmitted to us or any third party:

What's storedWhy
Level progress & unlocked levelsSo you can pick up where you left off
Coin balance & owned power-upsYour in-game currency and inventory
Statistics (combo, accuracy, time played, etc.)Shown on your Statistics screen; you can reset these anytime
Achievement & daily reward progressTracks what you've unlocked and claimed
Sound, music, vibration & notification preferencesRemembers your Settings choices
What this means for you: if you uninstall the App, or clear its storage from Android's Settings, this data is permanently deleted — we have no copy of it, because we never received one.

📺 Advertising & AdMob

Burst the Bubble shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the game free — specifically, rewarded video ads (for bonus coins or a second chance) and interstitial ads shown between levels.

To serve and measure these ads, Google AdMob's SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (which you can reset or opt out of in your device settings)
  • General device information (device type, OS version, language, general region)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, clicked, or completed)

This data is collected and processed by Google under Google's own privacy and advertising policies, not by us. You can review how Google handles this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses advertising data
  • Google Ads Settings — manage or opt out of personalized ads

🔐 App Permissions

Burst the Bubble requests the minimum permissions needed to run and show ads:

PermissionPurpose
Internet accessLoading ads and, if enabled, checking for app updates
Network stateChecking connectivity before attempting to load an ad

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or location.

🧒 Children's Privacy

Burst the Bubble is designed as a general-audience casual game and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age — which is straightforward here, since the App doesn't collect personal information from any user in the first place.

🗑️ Data Retention & Deletion

Because all gameplay data lives locally on your device, you are always in control of it:

  • Reset your stats: use the "Reset Stats" option on the in-app Statistics screen at any time.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the App, or clear its storage via Android Settings → Apps → Burst the Bubble → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Advertising data: managed entirely by Google — see the Advertising section above for how to reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalization.

🛡️ Security

Since gameplay data is stored locally in the App's private, sandboxed storage area (standard Android app-storage protection, not accessible to other apps), it's protected by your device's own security — the same protection covering every other app on your phone. We recommend keeping your device's OS up to date and using a screen lock for the best protection of all data on your device.

✅ Your Choices

  • Ad personalization: opt out or manage ad topics anytime at adssettings.google.com, or reset your device's advertising ID in your device's privacy settings.
  • Notifications: toggle in-app notification preferences off anytime from the App's Settings screen, or disable notification permission entirely from Android Settings.
  • Sound & vibration: fully adjustable from the App's Settings screen.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage checking back periodically. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
