The Beginning

From a single idea to a studio

Shree Games & Apps was founded in 2025 with a simple but powerful idea: that a single motivated developer can build games that people genuinely love to play.

Starting with Google AI Studio prototypes and iterating rapidly, we've grown from a single game concept into a growing portfolio of Android experiences — each one more polished than the last.

Our studio is built on the belief that indie development isn't a limitation. It's a superpower. Small teams move fast, stay focused, and build with a level of care and personal investment that larger studios simply can't match.
