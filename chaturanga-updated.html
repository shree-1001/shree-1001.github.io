♟Information We Collect

We collect only what's needed to run your account, your matches, and the features you actively choose to use.

Account information

  • Email address and password (if you sign up directly), or your name and profile photo from Google Sign-In (if you use that option). We never see or store your Google password.
  • A display name and, optionally, a profile picture you upload (stored as a small compressed image tied to your account) or an emoji avatar you select.
  • Your country, provided once during onboarding, used to show you relevant leagues and opponents.

Gameplay information

  • Match history, moves, results, and ratings.
  • Progress data: league standing, streak, Pana and Diamonds balances, lessons completed, and badges earned.
  • Puzzle and campaign progress.

Social information

  • Your friends list and any friend requests you send or receive.
  • Messages you send in chat with friends.
  • A referral code associated with your account, and, if you used someone else's code to sign up, a record of that.

Device and usage information

  • General app usage and interaction data via Firebase Analytics (e.g. which features are used, session length) — this is aggregated and not used to build an advertising profile of you outside this app.
  • Basic device information (device model, OS version, app version) for crash diagnostics and compatibility.

♞How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign back in.
  • To run gameplay: matchmaking, move validation, scoring, leagues, and rewards.
  • To power features you opt into: friends, chat, and finding contacts already on the app.
  • To show ads (see Advertising below) and, where you've earned it, credit in-app rewards (Energy or Diamonds) for watching one.
  • To understand which parts of the app are actually used, so we can improve it.
  • To detect and respond to abuse, cheating, or violations of our terms.
We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your chat messages, match history, or friend list for advertising purposes.

♗Friends, Chat & Contact Sync

Chat

Messages you send to friends are delivered and stored so your conversation history is available across your devices. Only you and the friend in that conversation can read those messages.

Adding a friend from your contacts (fully optional)

If you use "Add a Friend from Contacts," here's exactly what happens — this feature never requires broad contacts access, only your phone's own contact picker:

  • Tapping the button opens your phone's own built-in contacts picker — the same system screen any app uses to let you pick one contact. Chaturanga is never given access to your full contact list, only whichever single contact you choose to hand over.
  • That one phone number is converted into a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) on your device, before anything is sent anywhere.
  • Only that hash — never the real phone number, yours or your contact's — is checked against other users who've also enabled discovery.
  • We cannot reverse a hash back into a phone number, and we never store your contact's name, photo, or any other contact details.
  • Want to check more than one person? Just tap the button again and pick another — each pick is a separate, deliberate choice, not an automatic bulk scan.

♘Advertising & Your Ad Choices

Chaturanga shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob:

  • Interstitial ads — a full-screen ad shown right after a match ends.
  • Rewarded ads — optional ads you choose to watch in exchange for Energy or Diamonds. Watching is always your choice; declining never blocks core gameplay.

Consent (EU/UK/applicable US states)

If you're in a region where consent is legally required (including the EEA, UK, and applicable US states), you'll see a consent form the first time you open the app, before any personalized ad is requested. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Options (Ad Consent).

Declining personalized ads doesn't reduce the rewards you earn from watching a rewarded ad — you may simply see a less targeted ad.

♛How Information Is Shared

  • With other players: your username, avatar, league, streak, badges, and top-league finishes are visible to other signed-in users (this is how leaderboards and public profiles work). Your email is never shown to other players.
  • With service providers: the third parties listed above, strictly to operate the features described in this policy.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Chaturanga, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: if Chaturanga is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may transfer as part of that deal, under the same protections described here.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

♙Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and its associated data at any time from Profile → Reset Profile, or by contacting us using the details below. Some information may be retained briefly afterward where we're legally required to (for example, financial or fraud records), but it is removed from active use.

♜Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our servers, database rules that restrict who can read or write which data, and Firebase App Check to help verify that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

♝Children's Privacy

Chaturanga is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

♚Your Rights

If you're in the EU/UK/EEA (GDPR)

  • Right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Right to restrict or object to certain processing, and to data portability.
  • Right to withdraw consent (for ads) at any time without affecting prior processing.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you're in California or another applicable US state (CCPA/CPRA)

  • Right to know what personal information we collect and how it's used.
  • Right to request deletion of your personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell personal information, and you can manage ad-related choices in Settings.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.
